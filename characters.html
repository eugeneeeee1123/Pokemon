Gyms

Gyms are regional testing facilities run by type specialists. Each Gym tests whether a challenger understands type matchups and team balance instead of relying on a single favorite Pokémon.

Defeating a Gym Leader earns an official Badge. Collecting eight Badges qualifies a Trainer for the regional Pokémon League and keeps traded Pokémon obedient in battle.
