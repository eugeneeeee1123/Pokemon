世界观与时空背景 / World Setting & Chronology

A background reference for how Pokémon regions, history, and spacetime fit together. This guide separates in-game lore from real-world release history, and notes canon sources to prevent mixing game and anime storylines.

In-Universe Game Canon

Source: Core video games developed by Game Freak. This continuity does not follow the animated television series, which uses an unaging floating timeline where Ash Ketchum remains ten years old across twenty-five seasons.

Regions and Spatial Geography

The first four regions (Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, and Sinnoh) sit in close geographic proximity, based on the real-world Japanese regions of Kanto, Kansai, Kyushu, and Hokkaido. Kanto and Johto share a contiguous land border connected by Route 27, Tohjo Falls, and the Magnet Train between Saffron City and Goldenrod City. Later titles moved to overseas territories: Unova is based on the New York metropolitan area, Kalos on northern France, Alola on Hawaii, Galar on the United Kingdom, and Paldea on the Iberian Peninsula. For regional borders, native dex slices, and field survey maps from Kanto #001 to Paldea #1025, see the Regions index.

Chronology from Ancient Eras to Modern Leagues

The core series establishes chronological milestones through in-game history and NPC dialogue:

  • Ancient history: Pokémon Legends: Arceus documents the settlement of ancient Sinnoh (Hisui) and the earliest hand-crafted wooden apricorn Poké Balls. Three thousand years before modern events, an ancient war in Kalos ended when the king AZ fired the ultimate weapon powered by Pokémon life energy.
  • Timeline split: Dialogue during the Delta Episode in Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire confirmed that AZ firing the ultimate weapon split the games into two separate branches: an original timeline without Mega Evolution (Generation I to Generation V), and an alternate timeline where Mega Evolution exists.
  • Modern league era: Generation I (Red, Green, Blue) and Generation III (Ruby, Sapphire) happen at roughly the same point in time. Three years later, Generation II (Gold, Silver) and Generation IV (Diamond, Pearl) run concurrently. Generation V (Black and White) takes place several years later, with Black 2 and White 2 and Generation VI (X and Y) occurring two years after that. Later adventures in Alola, Galar, and Paldea proceed forward in modern sequence.

Anomalous Spacetime Phenomena

The game universe features specific localized breaches in space and time:

  • Space-time rifts: Atmospheric tears over Mount Coronet in ancient Hisui, triggered by Giratina destabilizing the Temple of Sinnoh, which caused items and people from different centuries to fall through.
  • The Distortion World: A parallel plane where Giratina was banished. Normal gravity, surface orientation, and the standard forward flow of time do not apply there.
  • Ultra Wormholes: Interdimensional gateways in Alola opened by Solgaleo, Lunala, or Necrozma. These tunnels connect our world to extradimensional habitats such as Ultra Deep Sea and the ruined civilization of Ultra Megalopolis.
  • Area Zero and Paradox Pokémon: The Great Crater of Paldea houses a functioning time machine built by Professor Sada or Professor Turo. The machine extracted ancient past or distant future Paradox specimens into the present day.

现实系列演进 / Franchise Milestones

The real-world development of the franchise spans thirty years of hardware changes:

  • 1996: Game Freak and Nintendo released Pocket Monsters Red and Green for the Game Boy in Japan, directed by Satoshi Tajiri with artwork by Ken Sugimori. This established the initial 151 species indexed on this site.
  • 1998-1999: International localized releases as Pokémon Red and Blue established the global franchise.
  • Hardware generations: The series transitioned across Game Boy Color (1999, Gen II), Game Boy Advance (2002, Gen III), Nintendo DS (2006, Gen IV), Nintendo 3DS (2013, Gen VI), and Nintendo Switch (2019, Gen VIII).
  • Data compatibility: The 2002 release of Ruby and Sapphire rebuilt the core internal data structures for individual Pokémon (introducing 31-point Individual Values and personality values). Because of this unified structure, a Pokémon caught on a Game Boy Advance cartridge in 2002 can still be transferred upward through the DS, 3DS, and Pokémon Bank into Pokémon HOME on the Nintendo Switch today.
